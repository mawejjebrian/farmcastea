Farm action guidance

Demo advisory
Before heavy rain: Check drainage channels, protect harvested produce from moisture, and avoid applying fertiliser immediately before expected runoff.
If a dry spell develops: Prioritise watering for young plants, mulch where suitable, and monitor soil moisture rather than relying on a calendar alone.
For planting decisions: Do not plant based on this demo. Check an updated local forecast and seek advice from an agricultural extension worker.

How the service works

  1. Collect verified weather and climate information.
  2. Translate it into practical, locally relevant advice.
  3. Share it through this website and WhatsApp.
  4. Gather farmer feedback and improve the service.

This version demonstrates the user journey. Live weather feeds, automated WhatsApp delivery and validated agronomic advisories are not yet connected.

FARMCAST EA is an early-stage concept prototype. It is not an emergency alert service and does not replace official forecasts, warnings or professional agricultural advice.
